================================================================================
                      IMPORTANT FACTORS FOR NBA PLAYER PROPS
                         Reference Guide for Model Development
================================================================================

This document outlines ALL critical factors to consider when analyzing NBA
player props. These factors are derived from Idea.txt and real-world analysis.

================================================================================
                         TIER 1: CRITICAL FACTORS
                    (Must be considered for every pick)
================================================================================

1. PLAYER BASELINE STATISTICS
   -------------------------
   - Season average (most stable predictor)
   - Last 10 games average (recent form)
   - Last 5 games average (immediate trend)
   - Standard deviation / consistency
   - Minutes played average

   KEY FINDING: Sportsbook lines closely match season averages rounded to 0.5

2. INJURY STATUS
   -------------
   - Is the player injured? (DNP = no pick)
   - Coming back from injury? (Expect rust, reduced minutes)
   - First game back vs 2nd/3rd game back
   - Minutes restrictions (CRITICAL - reduces all stats proportionally)
   - Teammate injuries (affects usage/opportunities)
   - Should also consider the upside for the other team, depending on that player that is injured.

   IMPORTANT: Players returning from injury are HIGH RISK
   - First game: Expect 70-80% of normal production
   - Weeks 1-2: Gradual ramp-up
   - Watch for minutes limits

3. BACK-TO-BACK GAMES (B2B)
   ------------------------
   - Second night of B2B = fatigue factor
   - Expect 5-10% reduction in production
   - Veterans more affected than young players
   - Watch for "rest days" (star players sit)
   
   FINDING: B2B factor has 86.7% hit rate when used correctly

4. MINUTES PROJECTION
   ------------------
   - Expected minutes vs average minutes
   - Blowout risk (winning/losing by 15+ = bench time)
   - Rotation changes
   - Foul trouble history
   
   RULE: Stats scale roughly linearly with minutes

================================================================================
                         TIER 2: MATCHUP FACTORS
                    (Analyze opponent for adjustment)
================================================================================

5. OPPONENT DEFENSE VS POSITION
   ----------------------------
   - Elite defense (top 5) = reduce projection 5-10%
   - Good defense (6-10) = reduce projection 3-5%
   - Average defense (11-20) = no adjustment
   - Poor defense (21-25) = increase projection 3-5%
   - Terrible defense (26-30) = increase projection 5-10%

   Track separately for:
   - Points allowed to position
   - Rebounds allowed to position
   - Assists allowed to position

6. PACE OF PLAY
   ------------
   - Fast-paced teams = more possessions = more opportunities
   - Slow teams = fewer possessions
   - Adjust projections based on expected pace
   
   FORMULA: Adjusted = Base * (Matchup_Pace / League_Avg_Pace)

7. HOME/AWAY SPLITS
   ----------------
   - Some players perform better at home
   - Consider travel fatigue (West coast to East coast)
   - Altitude adjustment (Denver games)
   
   NOTE: Home advantage is ~3 points for team, ~1-2% for individuals

================================================================================
                         TIER 3: SITUATIONAL FACTORS
                    (Check when applicable)
================================================================================

8. COLD STREAKS / HOT STREAKS
   --------------------------
   COLD STREAK INDICATORS:
   - Below average in 3+ consecutive games
   - Shooting percentage down
   - Plus/minus negative
   
   CAUTION: Don't over-rely on "cold bounce" theory
   - Cold streak could indicate underlying issue:
     * Injury not disclosed
     * Trade rumors / distractions
     * Coaching/rotation changes
     * Personal issues

   HOT STREAK INDICATORS:
   - Above average in 3+ consecutive games
   - Shooting percentage up
   - Plus/minus positive
   
   NOTE: Hot streaks tend to regress faster than cold streaks

9. SCHEDULE CONTEXT
   ----------------
   - Third game in four nights (3-in-4)
   - End of road trip fatigue
   - Before/after All-Star break
   - Playoff push vs tanking team
   - Rivalry games (higher intensity)

10. TEAM GAME SCRIPT EXPECTATION
    ---------------------------
    - Expected close game = starters play more
    - Expected blowout (either direction) = risk of reduced minutes
    - Underdog = may garbage time stats
    
    SPREAD GUIDELINE from Idea.txt:
    - Target games with spread < 6 points (closer games)

================================================================================
                         TIER 4: PLAYER-SPECIFIC FACTORS
                    (Build player profiles over time)
================================================================================

11. PLAYER ARCHETYPES
    -----------------
    Centers:
    - Point centers (playmakers) - more assists
    - Stretch fives (shooters) - more 3PA, variable scoring
    - Rim runners (vertical threats) - consistent around rim
    - Two-way centers - defense affects rebounds
    
    Guards:
    - Score-first guards - volume shooters
    - Pass-first guards - assist heavy
    - 3-and-D guards - consistent but lower volume
    
    Forwards:
    - Stretch fours - 3-point heavy
    - Traditional PFs - post scoring, rebounds
    - Point forwards - playmaking, assists

12. STAR PLAYER STATUS
    ------------------
    Definition (from Idea.txt):
    - Average 23+ minutes per game, OR
    - Starter when key players are out
    
    Why it matters:
    - Star players have higher floors
    - More consistent usage
    - Don't target deep bench players

13. HEAD-TO-HEAD HISTORY
    -------------------
    - How does player perform vs specific team?
    - Some players exploit certain defenses
    - Personal rivalries can boost performance
    
    SAMPLE SIZE: Need 3+ games for reliability

================================================================================
                         TIER 5: LINE-RELATED FACTORS
                    (How we compare to sportsbook)
================================================================================

14. LINE SOURCE
    -----------
    - Sportsbook line (real market) vs Derived line (our calculation)
    - Sportsbook lines are harder to beat (sharper)
    - Derived lines may be "easier" (less efficient)
    
    KEY INSIGHT: Models perform better against derived lines
    - V16: 75% SB vs 67% Derived
    - This suggests true edge is lower than backtests show

15. LINE MOVEMENT
    -------------
    - If line moves toward our projection = confirmation
    - If line moves away = smart money disagrees
    - Large moves (1+ points) indicate significant info

16. EDGE CALCULATION
    ----------------
    Edge = (Projection - Line) / Line * 100
    
    Minimum edges by line source:
    - Sportsbook lines: 8-10% minimum
    - Derived lines: 12-15% minimum (higher bar)

================================================================================
                    FACTORS TO AVOID (RED FLAGS)
================================================================================

1. AVOID relying solely on "cold bounce" without other factors
   - Could be injury/personal issue not reported

2. AVOID late bench players
   - High variance, unpredictable minutes

3. AVOID assist props for players averaging < 4 APG
   - From Idea.txt: Too volatile

4. AVOID first-week players for new teams (trades)
   - Role not established yet

5. AVOID expecting immediate bounce from multi-game cold streak
   - Regression is gradual, not instant

================================================================================
                      FACTOR WEIGHTING GUIDE
================================================================================

When multiple factors conflict, prioritize:

1. Injury status (HIGHEST - can invalidate all other analysis)
2. Minutes projection
3. B2B / schedule fatigue
4. Season average baseline
5. Opponent defense
6. Recent form (L5, L10)
7. Head-to-head history
8. Situational factors (LOWEST - use as tiebreaker)

================================================================================
                      CONFIDENCE SCORING
================================================================================

PREMIUM CONFIDENCE (Best picks):
- 4+ positive factors aligned
- No major red flags
- Edge > 15% with sportsbook line
- Player is established star

HIGH CONFIDENCE:
- 3+ positive factors
- Minor concerns only
- Edge > 10%
- Solid sample size

STANDARD CONFIDENCE:
- 2+ positive factors
- Some uncertainty
- Edge > 8%
- Use for volume plays

PASS (Don't bet):
- Conflicting factors
- Major red flags
- Edge < 8%
- Insufficient data
