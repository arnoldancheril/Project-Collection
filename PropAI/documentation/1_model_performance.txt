                       KEY LEARNINGS
================================================================================

1. WALK-FORWARD BACKTESTING IS CRITICAL
   - Traditional backtests may leak future data
   - Our walk-forward approach uses only pre-date data
   - This gives honest, reproducible results

2. SPORTSBOOK RATE IS THE TRUE MEASURE
   - Derived line performance is often inflated
   - V15 shows 67.5% vs derived but only 36.4% vs sportsbook
   - Always check the SB vs Der gap to identify inflation

3. HONEST MODELS IDENTIFIED
   - RCM: +9.5% gap (SB > Der) - Shows real edge
   - V14 Under: +9.9% gap - Reliable UNDER plays
   - V16 General: +5.6% gap - Top overall performer
   - V9: +3.9% gap - Consistent baseline

4. LINE PROJECTION MODEL WORKS
   - Season average projections used in walk-forward testing
   - MAE of ~1.15 points vs actual sportsbook lines
   - More accurate than legacy L10×1.05 method

5. VOLUME VS QUALITY TRADEOFF
   - V16: 102 picks at 69.6% (high quality, low volume)
   - V19: 1,313 picks at 55.9% (high volume, lower quality)
   - Choose based on bankroll and risk tolerance

6. UNDER MODELS SHOW PROMISE

================================================================================
                   WALK-FORWARD METHODOLOGY NOTES
================================================================================

This backtest implements proper walk-forward methodology as documented in
WALK_FORWARD_BACKTESTING.md:

1. DATE ISOLATION: For each test date D, only data from dates < D is used
2. SEQUENTIAL PROCESSING: Dates processed in order, new data incorporated
3. LINE PROJECTION: Season averages calculated using only pre-date games
4. GRADING: Picks compared against actual outcomes
5. SOURCE TRACKING: Sportsbook vs Projected vs Derived tracked separately

- Season averages: Calculated from games before Dec 15
- Picks generated: Using Dec 15 game schedule
